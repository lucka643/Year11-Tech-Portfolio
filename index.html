Before you start

There are two ways to read this site.

Up in the top right corner there is a switch. Turn it on and the whole site swaps to a simpler, easier to read version. The design sketches become big photos you just scroll past, and the tabbed panels turn into plain tables and lists.

Leave it off and you get the full interactive version. You can change your mind whenever you like, and the site remembers which one you picked.
